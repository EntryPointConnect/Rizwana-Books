Green Juice & Gunmetal Gray

About the Book

Green Juice & Gunmetal Gray is a thriller of ambition, power, secrets, and survival.

When appearances are carefully constructed and hidden motives begin to surface, the line between success and danger becomes increasingly difficult to see.

The Story

Behind polished lives and powerful institutions are secrets that someone will do almost anything to protect. As the characters move deeper into a world of ambition, money, and deception, trust becomes a dangerous commodity.

What begins as a search for answers becomes a struggle for survival. Every decision has consequences, and the truth may be more dangerous than the people trying to hide it.

Themes

  • Power and ambition
  • Secrets and deception
  • Money and influence
  • Trust and betrayal
  • Survival

About the Author

Rizwana Khan is an author, educator, and writer whose work explores identity, power, social justice, migration, culture, and the complicated choices people make when their worlds begin to change.
