Azadi After Midnight

About the Book

Azadi After Midnight is a cyber noire about freedom, political change, identity, courage, and the difficult choices in the digital world.

Freedom is celebrated in public, but personal freedom remains complicated behind closed doors.

The Story

Against a backdrop of political uncertainty and social change, the characters must confront questions about the meaning of freedom.

As events unfold after midnight, private decisions begin to collide with public events. The characters discover that achieving freedom can be only the beginning of the struggle.

Themes

  • Freedom and independence
  • Political and social change
  • Identity and belonging
  • Family and loyalty
  • Courage and personal choice

About the Author

Rizwana Khan is an author, educator, and writer whose work explores identity, social justice, migration, culture, and the experiences of people navigating different worlds.
