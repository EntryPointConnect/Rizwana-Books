Mina and the Bees

About the Book

Mina and the Bees is a novel about family, migration, identity, belonging, and finding home.

Mina's journey crosses countries and cultures as she searches for a place where she can understand who she wants to become.

The Story

Mina's story begins with questions about identity. As her life takes her across different places and cultures, she must learn how to navigate the expectations of the people around her while discovering her own voice.

Through family relationships, friendship, migration, and encounters with a changing world, Mina begins to understand that home is not always a single place. Sometimes it is the people, memories, and experiences we carry with us.

Themes

  • Family and belonging
  • Migration and identity
  • Culture and heritage
  • Finding your own voice
  • Home and community

About the Author

Rizwana Khan is an author, educator, and writer whose work explores identity, social justice, migration, culture, and the experiences of people navigating more than one world.
