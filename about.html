About the Author

Rizwana Khan is an author, educator, and writer whose work explores identity, family, social justice, migration, and the choices people make when cultures and worlds collide.

Her writing includes novels, children's literature, and stories that explore questions of belonging, courage, freedom, and human connection.

Writing

Rizwana writes across genres, including literary fiction, social justice fiction, and thrillers.
