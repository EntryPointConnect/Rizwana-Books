Qandeel

About the Book

Qandeel is a novel about identity, courage, family, freedom, and the struggle to create your own future.

Qandeel's story explores what happens when a woman begins questioning the expectations placed upon her and decides to find her own voice.

The Story

Qandeel is a woman caught between family expectations, cultural traditions, and her own dreams. As she confronts difficult choices, she must decide how much of her life will be determined by others and how much she is willing to claim for herself.

Her journey is one of courage and self-discovery. Along the way, relationships, family history, and the pressures of society force her to reconsider what freedom really means.

Themes

  • Identity and self-discovery
  • Family and expectations
  • Women and independence
  • Cultural identity
  • Courage and freedom

About the Author

Rizwana Khan is an author, educator, and writer whose work explores identity, social justice, migration, culture, and the experiences of people navigating different worlds.
